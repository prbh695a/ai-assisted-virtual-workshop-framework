# AI-Assisted Virtual Workshop Framework

## Overview

This portfolio project demonstrates a reusable workshop framework I designed
to help distributed teams identify current challenges, generate ideas,
structure discussions, prioritise improvements and convert them into
actionable outcomes.

The framework combines:

- structured facilitation
- asynchronous training
- digital whiteboarding
- AI-assisted summarisation
- human validation
- reusable facilitator guidance

The objective was not simply to conduct one workshop, but to design a
process that could continue even when the original designer was unavailable.

## Why I Designed It

A workshop had to proceed while changing priorities meant that I could not
be physically present.

Instead of creating a dependency on the original facilitator, I created a
reusable set of short videos and structured guidance so that other
facilitators could understand the intent, run the individual stages and
maintain consistency.

This became an experiment in asynchronous facilitation and scalable
knowledge transfer.

## Workshop Architecture

### Part 1 – Understand and Structure

1. Current Situation
2. Brainwriting
3. Clustering

The priority is to collect perspectives from all relevant participants
before moving into decision-making.

### Part 2 – Decide and Act

4. Prioritisation
5. Action Definition
6. Closing and Ownership

If workshop time is limited, Part 1 can be completed with the wider group
and Part 2 can later be completed by a smaller decision-making group.

## AI-Assisted Layer

AI-assisted whiteboarding can support:

- clustering ideas
- identifying recurring themes
- preparing first-draft summaries
- reducing documentation effort

AI outputs are treated as drafts only.

Human review remains mandatory before results are accepted as the official
workshop outcome.

## Asynchronous Facilitation

To reduce dependency on one facilitator, I created short training modules:

| Module | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Introduction | Workshop objective and overall approach |
| Part 1 | Current Situation, Brainwriting and Clustering |
| Part 2 | Prioritisation, Actions and Closing |
| AI-Assisted Whiteboard Guide | Responsible use of AI-assisted summarisation |
| Participant Guide – English | Whiteboard preparation for participants |
| Participant Guide – German | German-language participant preparation |
| Facilitator Practice Guide | Practice environment before running the workshop |

## Digital Twin

Some facilitator videos use an AI-generated digital representation / voice
of the author.

The workshop concept, structure, scripts and guidance were designed and
reviewed by me. The digital representation was used only as a delivery
mechanism for asynchronous knowledge transfer.

## Design Principles

- Reduce single-person dependency
- Keep the process simple before adding technology
- Participation before prioritisation
- Human-in-the-loop AI
- Transparent AI usage
- Clear ownership of decisions
- Reusable training material
- Scalable facilitation
- Continuous improvement through participant feedback
